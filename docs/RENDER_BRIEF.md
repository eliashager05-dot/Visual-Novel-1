`src/assets/cg/` (illustrations). `.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp` and `.avif` all work. The game picks it up with no code
changes. Run `npm run renders` to check names and sizes.

**The protagonist is visible.** He appears in two-shots, over-the-shoulder shots and his own reaction shots.
Players name him themselves, so no render may show his name. His look is fixed: see his character sheet.

## Sets and lighting

15 physical sets cover all 20 locations. Build each set once and save a lighting preset per state.

### Car ferry deck · 10 shots

Stern deck of a small, old car ferry: wet painted steel, white railings, coiled rope, an orange life ring, a passenger-lounge wall with a digital clock. Open sea and the island beyond.

- **Ferry deck, dusk** (`ferry_deck_dusk`, 10 shots): Dusk at sea. Low orange sun behind cloud from screen right, cool blue shadows, warm rim light on faces and railings. Turns to blue hour by the end of the scene.

### Harbour and hill street · 6 shots

A small stone harbour with a ferry ramp, iron bollards and lamp posts; a steep cobbled street of whitewashed houses climbing to the hotel.

- **Halcyon Point harbour** (`harbour_dusk`, 6 shots): Last light. Sodium-warm harbour lamps as the key, deep blue sky fill, wet cobbles reflecting the lamps.

### The Halcyon, exterior · 10 shots

A four-storey 1920s seaside hotel of weathered white stone on a cliff top: glass porch, brass front door, hanging porch lamps, a neon sign reading THE HAL YON (the C is dead). Front steps down to the road.

- **The Halcyon, exterior** (`hotel_exterior_night`, 6 shots): Night. Warm light spilling from the porch and ground-floor windows, cool moonlight and sea mist. Before the storm: black clouds, wind, swinging lamps.
- **The Halcyon in the storm** (`hotel_exterior_storm`, 4 shots): Storm at night. Almost no ambient light: windows, lightning flashes and torch beams only. Heavy sideways rain, spray.

### Lobby · 15 shots

Black-and-white chequered tiles, a curling staircase, worn velvet armchairs, a fireplace, a reception desk with brass pigeonholes, a key cabinet, a clock and a green banker's lamp. One corridor is closed with a velvet rope and a sign: EAST WING CLOSED. A green leather guest book lies on the desk.

- **Lobby** (`hotel_lobby_evening`, 4 shots): Evening. Warm tungsten lamps and a low fire, pools of light with dark corners.
- **Lobby, after midnight** (`hotel_lobby_late`, 3 shots): After midnight. A single green banker's lamp on the reception desk; everything else in near darkness.
- **Lobby by candlelight** (`lobby_candlelight`, 8 shots): Power cut. Dozens of candles and one hurricane lamp: warm, low, flickering key light from below face height, deep shadows.

### Hotel kitchen · 5 shots

A big, old working kitchen: hanging copper pans, a huge cast-iron range, a scrubbed wooden table, steam.

- **Hotel kitchen** (`hotel_kitchen_night`, 5 shots): Bright warm overhead light, steam catching it, copper reflections.

### Bedroom corridor · 5 shots

A long corridor with wine-red carpet, brass room numbers, wall sconces and a tall window at the far end over the sea. The second-floor version ends in an unnumbered door.

- **First-floor corridor** (`hotel_corridor_night`, 5 shots): Dim wall sconces at intervals, cool night at the end window. In the power cut: a single candle.

### Dining room · 7 shots

A grand dining room built for a hundred, mostly dark. One long table near a large fireplace is set for six; the chair at the head stands slightly pulled out.

- **Dining room** (`dining_room_night`, 7 shots): Firelight from one side as the key, a few candles on the table, the rest of the room falling into darkness.

### Room 12 · 6 shots

A guest room: brass bed, writing desk under the window, faded wallpaper with blue herons, a cast-iron radiator, a nightstand. The window looks down on the cove and a lighthouse.

- **Room 12** (`player_room_night`, 6 shots): Night. Moonlight through the window and one bedside lamp. Final scene: pre-dawn blue, lamp off, a lit phone screen.

### The Low Tide · 10 shots

A vaulted stone cellar bar under the hotel: a bar made from dark ship's timber, shelves of bottles, fairy lights tangled in old fishing nets, small tables, stone stairs up to a street door, and a battered upright piano with a taped sign: PLEASE DON'T.

- **The Low Tide bar** (`low_tide_bar_night`, 8 shots): Open. Amber back-bar lights, fairy lights as bokeh, warm haze, cool night air at the top of the stairs.
- **The Low Tide, after closing** (`low_tide_bar_closed`, 2 shots): Closed. Fairy lights and one lamp on the piano; chairs up on tables, most of the room dark.

### The cove · 8 shots

A small sand-and-rock cove at the foot of the cliff, reached by a steep path. Black rocks, kelp, rock pools. Fifty metres out stands the rusted iron ring of an old mooring, exposed only at low tide.

- **The cove at night** (`cove_night`, 3 shots): Clear night with stars. Light comes from a head torch and from blue-green bioluminescence in the water, glowing wherever it is disturbed.
- **The cove at low tide** (`cove_low_tide_day`, 5 shots): Overcast afternoon, flat silver light, wet reflective mud and rock. Ends with a black storm front arriving over the sea.

### Terrace · 5 shots

A stone terrace hanging over the cliff: wrought-iron tables and chairs, a low wall, the open sea below. A doorway leads back into the hotel.

- **Terrace, morning** (`hotel_terrace_morning`, 5 shots): Bright, clear morning sun from the sea side; crisp shadows, glittering water.

### Coast road and car · 2 shots

A narrow road round the island above the sea, and the interior of a dark modern rental car seen from the passenger seat.

- **The coast road** (`island_road_day`, 2 shots): Daylight through car windows; clear at midday, then storm cloud and first rain on the drive back.

### Fish factory and boat shed · 3 shots

A derelict fish factory with a half-collapsed roof and rusted machinery; beside it, a small boat shed with a faded blue door and MARLOWE painted above it.

- **The old fish factory** (`fish_factory_day`, 3 shots): Flat grey overcast, soft shadowless light, muted colour.

### Boathouse and cliff steps · 5 shots

A stone boathouse at the foot of a hundred steps cut into the cliff: large wooden sea doors, a side door, a slipway. On the slipway sits an old blue wooden lobster boat with ELIN painted on the stern in white.

- **Boathouse, storm** (`boathouse_storm`, 5 shots): Storm at night. Torch beams as the only key light, lightning through the doors, rain and sea spray in the air.

### Margit's room · 3 shots

A lived-in bedroom-study frozen in March: crowded bookshelves, a brass barometer, a pipe rack, a desk under the window covered in hand-drawn sea charts, photographs pinned above it.

- **Margit's room** (`margit_room_night`, 3 shots): Candlelight only: one or two candles, warm and close, the edges of the room dark.
