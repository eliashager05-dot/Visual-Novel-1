## Character reference sheets

Before any scene work, each character needs a locked reference: front, three-quarter and profile head shots,
plus a full-body turnaround in their default outfit, rendered under neutral studio light.

- **Vera Lindqvist** (29) — Tall, straight-backed. Ash-blonde hair always pinned up; one strand escapes when she is tired. Grey-blue eyes, a faint scar through her left eyebrow. Dresses in charcoal and navy, sleeves rolled exactly twice. Reading glasses she denies needing.
- **Noor Haddad** (26) — Medium height, warm brown skin, dark curls usually stuffed under a knotted scarf. Gold hoop in one ear only — she lost the other "somewhere meaningful". Rolled-sleeve work shirts, a bar towel permanently over one shoulder, ink smudges on her fingers from writing lyrics on receipts.
- **Dr. Juniper Castell** (31) — Petite, freckled, copper-red hair cut short and usually damp. Oversized wool jumpers, a waterproof watch, salt stains on everything. Wears field boots to dinner. Bites her lip when calculating.
- **Sienna Marlowe** (33) — Dark hair in a sharp bob, olive skin, deep red lipstick that never smudges. Tailored camel coat, silk blouses, heels she somehow walks in on cobblestones. A cheap thread bracelet on her wrist that does not match anything.
- **Bram Okafor** (61) — Broad, bald, grey beard, forearms covered in old burn scars. Apron over a fisherman's jumper.
